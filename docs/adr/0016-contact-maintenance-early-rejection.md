## Interpretation and remaining work

Complete, equivalent rendered task evidence plus no strict file-count
improvement is a conclusive failure of this candidate's mandatory maintenance
objective. Record the narrow failure and retain Go. Missing, inconsistent or
failed rendering is **inconclusive**, never a negative file-count measurement
or a reason to lower the floor. If the objective improves, this check grants no
adoption: the complete reviewed runner, all three paired trials, every budget,
protected holdout and same-state rollback remain mandatory before adoption.

This is presentation-only evidence, not authentication, browser interaction or
accessibility, timing, RSS, clean-build or rollback proof. Mark every unperformed
dimension as unperformed. It changes neither the original thresholds nor the
2026-10-19T02:00:00Z–2026-10-26T02:00:00Z observation window and enables no schedule.
A later changed candidate, task, authority or dependency requires a separately
reviewed amendment, not tuning against this result.

A recorded early failure does not automatically close the full collector or
portal programme. Reconcile their outstanding scope explicitly, preserving
evidence and filing any still-needed delivery/removal work. No renderer code is
removed by this amendment. Production stays on Go and every private release
gate stays default-off; identity/provider/deployment/payment/legal prerequisites
remain independent.
